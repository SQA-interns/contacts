# US-012 Delete a Contact and restore it from Trash

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

Format: `general/requirements-standard.md`.

## Story

As an Owner or Editor, I want deleting a Contact to move it to Trash where I can restore it, so that a mistake does not lose data.

## Purpose

"No data is lost silently" is a principle of the product (Q-45). A Contact deleted by mistake must come back with everything on it. Priority and MVP are [ASSUMPTION]: the interview gives no priority for Trash; journey 6 of Q-54 and the project done criteria need it.

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Trash view | The page of a Group that lists its Contacts in Trash with name, deletion time, the user who deleted and the days left. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-012-01 | When an Owner or Editor asks to delete a Contact, the system shall ask for confirmation. | Q-38 |
| R-012-02 | When an Owner or Editor confirms the deletion, the system shall move the Contact to the Trash of its Group and hide it from the list, search, filters and export (BR-04). | Q-38 |
| R-012-03 | When an Owner or Editor opens the Trash view, the system shall list the Contacts in Trash of that Group with the days left before permanent deletion. | Q-38 |
| R-012-04 | When an Owner or Editor restores a Contact that has been in Trash for less than 30 days, the system shall return it to the Group with all its fields, Tags, Attributes and version number. | Q-38 |
| R-012-05 | If a Contact has been in Trash for 30 days or more, then the system shall not restore it and shall answer a restore request as "not found". | Q-38 |
| R-012-06 | If someone tries to change a Contact in Trash, then the system shall refuse and change nothing. | Q-38 |
| R-012-07 | When a Contact is deleted and when it is restored, the system shall record a History entry each (BR-03). | Q-37, Q-54 |
| R-012-08 | If a Viewer tries to delete a Contact, open the Trash view or restore a Contact, then the system shall refuse (BR-02). | Q-08 |
| R-012-09 | If a delete request concerns a Contact that is already in Trash, then the system shall change nothing and record nothing. | Q-38 |

## Out of scope

Deleting a Contact forever and the automatic deletion after 30 days (US-013); deleting a Group (US-020); bulk delete; restoring a Tag removed meanwhile.
