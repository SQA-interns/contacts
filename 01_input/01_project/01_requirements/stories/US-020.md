# US-020 Delete a Group

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

Format: `general/requirements-standard.md`.

## Story

As an Owner, I want to delete a Group I no longer need, so that its Contacts do not stay in the system forever.

## Purpose

Projects end. Their Contacts are personal data and must not stay without a reason. Deleting a whole Group is the most destructive action of the product, so it is confirmed by typing the name and goes to Trash first. Priority and MVP are [ASSUMPTION]: the interview names the action but gives no priority.

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Group Trash | The place where a deleted Group stays for 30 days, visible only to its Owners. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-020-01 | When an Owner types the exact name of the Group (after trimming spaces, same upper and lower case) and confirms, the system shall move the Group to the Group Trash. | Q-38 |
| R-020-02 | If the typed name differs from the Group name, then the system shall not delete the Group and shall keep the confirm button disabled or refuse the request. | Q-38 |
| R-020-03 | While a Group is in the Group Trash, the system shall answer every request about it from a Member as "not found" and remove it from the Group list (BR-04). | Q-38 |
| R-020-04 | While a Group is in the Group Trash, the system shall list it for its Owners with the days left before permanent deletion. | Q-38 |
| R-020-05 | When a Group has been in the Group Trash for 30 days, the system shall delete it permanently with all its Contacts, Tags, Attributes, Versions, History, Members and Invitations (BR-04). | Q-38 |
| R-020-06 | If an Editor or Viewer tries to delete the Group, then the system shall refuse (BR-02). | Q-38 |

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given | When | Then | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-020-01 | Ana owns `Acme` | She types `Acme` in the confirmation and confirms | `Acme` leaves her Group list and appears in her Group Trash with "30 days left" | R-020-01, R-020-04 |
| AC-020-02 | Same | She types `acme` | The Group is not deleted | R-020-02 |
| AC-020-03 | `Acme` is in the Group Trash; Boris was an Editor | Boris requests the Contacts of `Acme` | "Not found", no data | R-020-03 |
| AC-020-04 | `Acme` has been in the Group Trash for 30 days | The clean-up has run | Group, Contacts, History, Members and Invitations are gone from the database | R-020-05 |
| AC-020-05 | Boris is an Editor | He sends the delete request | Refused; `Acme` unchanged | R-020-06 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-020-01 | Name typed with leading or trailing spaces | Accepted after trimming | R-020-01 |
| EX-020-02 | Group name of 100 characters | Typed confirmation works | R-020-01 |
| EX-020-03 | Another Owner of the Group opens the Group Trash | Sees the Group too | R-020-04 |
| EX-020-04 | A Member is editing a Contact when the Group is deleted | The save is answered "not found" | R-020-03 |
| EX-020-05 | Group in the Group Trash for 29 days 23 hours | Still present in the Group Trash | R-020-05 |

## Out of scope

Deleting a Group forever before the 30 days; renaming; archiving a Group without deleting.
