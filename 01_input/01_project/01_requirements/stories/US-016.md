# US-016 See the History of a Contact

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

Format: `general/requirements-standard.md`.

## Story

As an Owner or Editor, I want every change of a Contact recorded and shown in a History tab, so that I can see who changed what and when.

## Purpose

The customer asked for an audit trail ("revizijska sled") of all changes to Contacts. When data is wrong, the team must know who changed it, and nobody must be able to hide a change.

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| History tab | The tab of a Contact that lists the History entries of that Contact, newest first. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-016-01 | When a Contact is created, the system shall record a History entry with the time, the user and the stored values (BR-03). | Q-37 |
| R-016-02 | When a Contact is edited, the system shall record a History entry with the time, the user and the old and new value of every changed field, and no entry for unchanged fields (BR-03). | Q-37 |
| R-016-03 | When an Owner or Editor opens the History tab of a Contact, the system shall list its History entries, newest first, with time shown in Europe/Ljubljana (project-wide facts), the user's name and e-mail address, the action and the old and new values. | Q-37, Q-44 |
| R-016-04 | If a Viewer asks for the History of a Contact, then the system shall refuse (BR-02). | Q-37 |
| R-016-05 | If any request, including one by an Owner, tries to change or delete a History entry, then the system shall refuse it and change nothing. | Q-37, Q-45 |
| R-016-06 | The system shall keep the user's name and e-mail address in a History entry as they were at the time of the action, except where US-013 (values of a Contact deleted forever) and US-021 (e-mail of a deleted account) say otherwise. | Q-37, Q-47 |
| R-016-07 | The system shall keep the History entries of a Group as long as the Group exists [ASSUMPTION: subject to the open personal-data question OQ-013-01]. | Q-37 |
| R-016-08 | The system shall show the History entries of the Contact on the History tab also after the Contact is restored from Trash. | Q-54 |

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given | When | Then | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-016-01 | Ana creates `Ana Novak` | — | The History tab shows one entry "created" by Ana with the stored values | R-016-01, R-016-03 |
| AC-016-02 | `Ana Novak` has last name `Novak`, company `Acme` | Boris changes last name to `Kos` and leaves company | The newest entry shows Boris, last name `Novak` → `Kos`, and no line for company | R-016-02 |
| AC-016-03 | An entry occurred at 2026-01-15 10:00 UTC and one at 2026-07-01 10:00 UTC | An Owner opens the tab with Slovene UI | They are shown as `15. 1. 2026 11:00` and `1. 7. 2026 12:00` | R-016-03 |
| AC-016-04 | Viola is a Viewer | She opens or requests the History tab | Refused | R-016-04 |
| AC-016-05 | An entry exists | The Owner sends a request to change or delete it (UI offers none) | Refused; the entry is unchanged | R-016-05 |
| AC-016-06 | Boris changed a Contact; Boris is later removed and his display name changed in Firebase | An Owner opens the tab | The entry still shows the name and e-mail he had at the time | R-016-06 |
| AC-016-07 | A Contact is deleted and restored | The tab is opened | All earlier entries and the delete and restore entries are shown | R-016-08 |
| AC-016-08 | `Ana Novak` was saved without changes | The tab is opened | No new entry | R-016-02 |
| AC-016-09 | The creation entry of a Contact is 400 days old and many later entries exist | The tab is opened and paged to the end | The creation entry is still listed | R-016-07 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-016-01 | Edit that changes only e-mail case (`ANA@x.si` typed over `ana@x.si`) | No change after lowercasing; no entry | R-016-02 |
| EX-016-02 | Edit changes a field from empty to a value | Entry shows old empty and the new value | R-016-02 |
| EX-016-03 | Edit changes 5 fields at once | One entry with 5 old/new pairs | R-016-02 |
| EX-016-04 | Entry at 2026-03-29 00:30 UTC (the night clocks go forward) | Shown as 01:30 (CET) and 2026-03-29 01:30 UTC as 03:30 (CEST) | R-016-03 |
| EX-016-05 | A History entry is requested by an id of another Group | Not found | R-016-03 |
| EX-016-06 | A Contact with 200 entries | All can be reached by paging | R-016-03 |
| EX-016-07 | Field value of 5,000 characters in notes | Old and new value are stored in full | R-016-02 |

## Out of scope

The Group activity list (US-017); Versions and restore (US-022, US-023); exporting History; retention of History after permanent deletion (OQ-013-01).
