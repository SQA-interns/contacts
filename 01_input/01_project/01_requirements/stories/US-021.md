# US-021 Delete my account

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

Format: `general/requirements-standard.md`.

## Story

As a User, I want to delete my own account, so that I can leave the product and my personal data is removed.

## Purpose

People leave companies and projects. Without a way out, their name and e-mail stay forever. Priority and MVP are [ASSUMPTION]: the interview names the action but gives no priority.

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sole Owner | A User who is the only Owner of a Group. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-021-01 | When a User confirms the deletion of their account, the system shall delete the User's Firebase account, the User record and all Memberships of the User. | Q-47 |
| R-021-02 | If the User is the Sole Owner of at least one Group, then the system shall delete nothing and list those Groups with the instruction to hand each over to another Member (US-015) or to delete it (US-020). | Q-47 |
| R-021-03 | When an account is deleted, the system shall keep the User's name in the History entries the User caused. | Q-47 |
| R-021-04 | When an account is deleted, the system shall keep the Contacts that the User created. | Q-47 |
| R-021-05 | When an account is deleted, the system shall not let anyone sign in with it; a person who registers again with the same e-mail address shall get a new account without Memberships. | Q-47 |
| R-021-06 | Before deleting the account, the system shall ask for confirmation that says the deletion cannot be undone. | Q-47 |

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given | When | Then | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-021-01 | Boris is an Editor in `Acme` | He confirms the deletion of his account | The account is gone; `Acme` no longer lists him as a Member | R-021-01, R-021-06 |
| AC-021-02 | Ana is the Sole Owner of `Acme` | She tries to delete her account | Nothing is deleted; `Acme` is listed with the instruction | R-021-02 |
| AC-021-03 | Ana made `Acme` have a second Owner | She deletes her account | It works | R-021-01 |
| AC-021-04 | Boris edited Contacts, then deleted his account | An Owner opens the History | His entries show his name | R-021-03 |
| AC-021-05 | Same | An Owner opens the Contacts he created | They exist unchanged | R-021-04 |
| AC-021-06 | Boris's account was deleted | Someone signs in with his old credentials | Sign-in fails | R-021-05 |
| AC-021-07 | Same | A person registers `boris@acme.com` again | A new account without Memberships | R-021-05 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-021-01 | A User who is Sole Owner of 2 Groups | Both are listed | R-021-02 |
| EX-021-02 | A User with no Group | Account deleted | R-021-01 |
| EX-021-03 | The confirmation is cancelled | Nothing changes | R-021-06 |
| EX-021-04 | A User with a pending Invitation to the User's address from another Group | The Invitation stays pending for the e-mail address [ASSUMPTION] | R-021-05 |

## Out of scope

Exporting the User's data; deleting the Contacts the User created; a grace period before deletion.
