# US-015 Manage Members and Roles

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

Format: `general/requirements-standard.md`.

## Story

As an Owner, I want to see the Members of my Group and change their Role or remove them, so that only the right people can see and change the Contacts.

## Purpose

People change projects and companies. An Owner must be able to take rights away at once, and the Group must never be left without an Owner. Priority and MVP are [ASSUMPTION]: the interview gives no priority; the Roles of Q-08 are useless without a way to change them.

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Member list | The page of a Group that lists its Members with display name, e-mail address and Role. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-015-01 | When a Member opens the Member list, the system shall show every Member of the Group with display name, e-mail address and Role [ASSUMPTION: all Members may see it]. | Q-08 |
| R-015-02 | When an Owner changes the Role of a Member, the system shall apply the new Role to the Member's next request. | Q-08 |
| R-015-03 | When an Owner removes a Member, the system shall remove the Member's access so that the Member's next request for the Group is answered "not found". | Q-08, Q-48 |
| R-015-04 | If a change or removal would leave the Group without an Owner, then the system shall refuse it and show "A group needs at least one owner". | Q-48 |
| R-015-05 | If an Editor or Viewer tries to change a Role or remove a Member, then the system shall refuse (BR-02). | Q-08 |
| R-015-06 | When a Role is changed or a Member is removed, the system shall record a History entry (BR-03). | Q-37 |
| R-015-07 | When a Member is removed, the system shall keep the Contacts and History entries that the Member created and shall keep the Member's name in the History. | Q-47 |
| R-015-08 | The system shall let no one see or manage a Group of which they are not a Member, including operators of the system through the product. | Q-27 |

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given | When | Then | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-015-01 | Ana (Owner), Boris (Editor), Viola (Viewer) | Viola opens the Member list | Three Members with names, e-mail addresses and Roles are shown, without change controls | R-015-01, R-015-05 |
| AC-015-02 | Same | Ana changes Boris to Viewer | Boris's next request to create a Contact is refused | R-015-02 |
| AC-015-03 | Same | Ana removes Boris | Boris's next request for `Acme` is "not found" and `Acme` is gone from his Group list | R-015-03 |
| AC-015-04 | Ana is the only Owner | Ana tries to change herself to Editor, then tries to remove herself | Both refused with "A group needs at least one owner" | R-015-04 |
| AC-015-05 | Ana and Cene are Owners | Ana changes herself to Editor | Accepted; Cene is the Owner | R-015-04 |
| AC-015-06 | Boris is an Editor | He sends a Role change | Refused | R-015-05 |
| AC-015-07 | A Role change and a removal | — | Two History entries exist with the Members and Roles | R-015-06 |
| AC-015-08 | Boris created a Contact and was removed | An Owner opens the Contact's History | The Contact exists; the entry shows Boris's name | R-015-07 |
| AC-015-09 | Boris is not a Member of `Acme` | He opens the Member list of `Acme` | "Not found"; no data | R-015-08 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-015-01 | Two Owners; both demote each other at the same time | One succeeds, the other is refused so one Owner remains | R-015-04 |
| EX-015-02 | Owner changes a Member to the Role the Member already has | Nothing changes; no History entry | R-015-02 |
| EX-015-03 | A Member is removed while editing a Contact | The save is refused as "not found" | R-015-03 |
| EX-015-04 | Remove a Member who is not in the Group | "Not found" | R-015-03 |
| EX-015-05 | A removed Member is invited again | Allowed as a new Invitation | R-015-03 |

## Out of scope

A Member leaving a Group by themselves; transferring ownership as a separate action (promote another Member to Owner, then change the first one); an in-app administrator (Q-27: none); deleting an account (US-021).
