# US-013 Delete a Contact forever

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

Format: `general/requirements-standard.md`.

## Story

As an Owner, I want to delete a Contact in Trash permanently, and I expect the system to do it by itself after 30 days, so that personal data does not stay longer than needed.

## Purpose

Contacts are personal data. The Trash protects against mistakes; it must not become a place where data stays forever. The Owner needs a way to remove a person's data at once. Priority and MVP are [ASSUMPTION]: the interview gives no priority; permanent deletion after 30 days is part of what the product promises about personal data (Q-46).

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Permanent deletion | Removal of the Contact with its fields, Tags links, Attributes and Versions, so that it can no longer be restored, found or exported. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-013-01 | When an Owner confirms "Delete forever" for a Contact in Trash, the system shall delete the Contact permanently, also before 30 days have passed. | Q-38 |
| R-013-02 | Before deleting forever, the system shall ask for confirmation that says the deletion cannot be undone. | Q-38 |
| R-013-03 | If an Editor or Viewer tries to delete a Contact forever, then the system shall refuse (BR-02) and keep the Contact in Trash. | Q-38, Q-08 |
| R-013-04 | When a Contact has been in Trash for 30 days, the system shall delete it permanently within 24 hours [ASSUMPTION: the interview gives no delay for the clean-up job] (BR-04). | Q-38 |
| R-013-05 | After permanent deletion, the system shall find the Contact in no list, search, export or Trash view and shall answer requests for it as "not found". | Q-38 |
| R-013-06 | When a Contact is deleted permanently, the system shall record a History entry that names the action, the user (or "system") and the time and contains no personal data of the Contact [ASSUMPTION]. | Q-37 |
| R-013-07 | After permanent deletion, the system shall keep the Tags of the Group. | Q-31 |
| R-013-08 | When a Contact is deleted permanently, the system shall keep its earlier History entries with time, user and action, and shall replace the old and new values of the Contact's fields in them with the text "removed" (developer answer to OQ-013-01). | Q-37, Q-38 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-013-01 | "Delete forever" on a Contact that is not in Trash | Refused; the Contact is unchanged | R-013-01 |
| EX-013-02 | Contact in Trash for exactly 30 days | Counts as expired (BR-04); deleted by the next clean-up | R-013-04 |
| EX-013-03 | Contact in Trash for 29 days 23 hours | Still in Trash | R-013-04 |
| EX-013-04 | Two Owners confirm at once | One deletion; the other gets "not found" | R-013-05 |
| EX-013-06 | A History entry of the Contact lists a merge with another Contact's values | Only the deleted Contact's values read "removed"; the other Contact's values stay | R-013-08 |
| EX-013-05 | The Group has Contacts in Trash and the Group is deleted (US-020) | Handled by US-020 | — |

## Out of scope

Deleting a Group; deleting a User's account; deleting backups (backups are kept 14 days, Q-50); a legal erasure procedure.
